Question:
What does a spiculated, periosteal reaction on a long bone (at metaphysis, near the end, but not AT the end) on xray mean in someone with knee pain?

Ans:
Osteosarcoma
<br><br>
-occurs at METAPHYSIS, ie. the neck of the bone (near end of the bone, but NOT the epiphysis)
