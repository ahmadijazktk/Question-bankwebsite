Question:
What does a biopsy of s-IBM show?

Ans:
Triad of inflammation, invasion of CD8+ lymphocytes of muscle fibers (with MHC I), cytoplasmic and intranuclear inclusions containing amyloid beta and other Alzheimer-type proteins, and segmental loss of cytochrome C oxidase activity in muscle fibers.

-can present with facial muscle involvement. CKs normal or mildly elevated
-***weakness of finger flexion and knee extension***
