Question:
According to the 2023 ACR guidelines for ILD, are the following recommended FOR or AGAINST as 1st line treatment for RP-ILD (from Systemic autoimmune rheumatic diseases)?

Methotrexate? {{………….}}
Leflunomide? {{………….}}
Azathioprine? {{………….}}
TNFi? {{………….}}
Abatacept? {{………….}}
Tocilizumab? {{………….}}
Nintedanib? {{………….}}
Pirfenidone? {{………….}}
PLEX? {{………….}}

Ans:
According to the 2023 ACR guidelines for ILD, are the following recommended FOR or AGAINST as 1st line treatment for RP-ILD (from Systemic autoimmune rheumatic diseases)?

Methotrexate? AGAINST 
Leflunomide? AGAINST
Azathioprine? AGAINST
TNFi? AGAINST
Abatacept? AGAINST
Tocilizumab? AGAINST
Nintedanib? AGAINST
Pirfenidone? AGAINST
PLEX? AGAINST

Essentially, the treatment options for RP-ILD are:

IV steroids, Rituximab, Cyclophosphamide, IVIG, MMF, CNIs, or JAKi

***if patient has MDA5 RP-ILD, there is a conditional recommendation for "triple therapy", which is:

IV steroids + 2 of the above options (Rituximab, Cyclophosphamide, IVIG, MMF, CNIs, or JAKi)
